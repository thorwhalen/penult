penult.images#

Images for the essay: find existing ones, generate a few, and keep the ledger.

Three sources, one ledger (images.md in the workspace, mirrored in the state):

  • search: existing stock, Creative-Commons and public-domain images through the illustration package, which carries licence and attribution with each hit;

  • generate: a paid image from a prompt through aix.image.generate_image, capped by the person’s max_generated_images so that an off prompt does not cost much; every prompt is kept in the ledger whether or not it was run;

  • add: an image the person made or found themselves, with its credit.

The searcher and the generator are seams: any callable of the same shape works, and tests pass fakes.

>>> credit_line({"title": "Harbour", "author": "A. Painter", "license": "cc-by", "source": "openverse"})
'Harbour, by A. Painter (cc-by, via openverse)'
